## Coach from report cards

1. Open an individual team member's report card for customer, dealer, product, activity, and follow-up detail.
2. Compare sales only within the same period and using the displayed source. Accounting sales are based on eligible Tally invoices.
3. Review invoice history and customer mapping before acting on a decline or inactivity alert.
4. Treat provisional thresholds as prompts for review, not approved performance policy.
5. Agree a specific next action with the salesperson and record it in the appropriate CRM follow-up or activity.

## Follow-ups and field activity

1. Review overdue follow-ups and planned visits for your team.
2. A past scheduled visit with no completion is awaiting confirmation; it does not establish that the visit was missed.
3. Ask the owner to update the visit outcome or explain the schedule change, then record the next action.
4. Use activity and attendance records for coaching. GPS may be unavailable or disabled; do not infer a location when none is recorded.

## Dealer and customer review

1. Check dealer sales against the reporting period and invoice history shown.
2. Confirm dealer/customer identity and RM ownership when records are unmapped or appear duplicated.
3. Escalate missing historical dates or mappings; the system must not invent new-customer dates or revenue.
4. Coordinate customer coverage changes with the administrator so access and reporting remain consistent.

## Ask AI responsibly

1. Ask focused questions about your team and an explicit period, such as current FY or month to date.
2. Review the cited figures against the dashboard/report card before communicating a decision.
3. Ask for clarification when data is stale, missing, or unmapped. AI output is advisory and can be unavailable when the provider is down.
4. Do not ask for another team's private data or include unnecessary customer personal information.

## Daily management routine

- Start with overdue customer commitments, then upcoming visits and open support requests.
- Prioritize a customer action with an owner and due date; avoid duplicate follow-ups.
- At day's end, confirm material visit and call outcomes are recorded and assign unresolved blockers.
- Escalate Tally sync failures, incorrect attribution, or access problems to the administrator.

## Report card terms

- **Net sales:** eligible committed Tally invoice amounts after credit-note effects; staged imports and Excel projections are excluded.
- **MTD / FYTD:** month to date / Indian financial year to date (year starts 1 April). Compare identical start and end dates.
- **Achievement:** actual sales divided by a valid target, multiplied by 100. Tally targets stored in lakh convert to rupees using 1 lakh = INR 100,000. Missing targets display unavailable.
- **Concentration:** the top five dealers' sales share of attributed total sales. A high share is a review prompt, not proof of customer risk.
- **Dormant / declining:** invoice inactivity across the configured threshold / lower sales than a comparable prior period with supporting history. No history is insufficient evidence, not proof of decline.
- **Visit completion:** recorded completed visits against scheduled visits. Check timing and owner explanations before judging outstanding visits.


## Account security - 21 October 2026

- Use your assigned Login ID or registered email with the same password. An explicitly requested professional account email can include your initial password and all saved employee details. Keep it private. New credentials work immediately on a reachable CRM; first-login password change is optional.
- To change your password voluntarily, open **My profile** > **Security** > **Change my password**. Enter current password, new password (12 to 200 characters) and confirmation.
- A successful change signs you out on all devices and cancels existing recovery links. Sign in again with the new password.
- Use **Forgot Password** for a 15-minute, single-use email reset link, or ask an administrator to reset your password. Never share your password.
