# PSSPL CRM SOP: RM / Sales Executive

Use the CRM to record work as it happens. Save visits, calls, leads, and follow-ups against the correct customer so your manager can review reliable activity.

## Sign in and sign out

1. Open the CRM address provided by your administrator and sign in with your assigned account.
2. Use **Sign out** in the sidebar when you finish or leave a shared device.
3. If the CRM returns you to Login, sign in again. Do not share your password or session.

## Start your day and attendance

1. Open **My Day** and review planned visits and open follow-ups.
2. Select **Check in** to record attendance. Allow location access if prompted; the CRM can record check-in without GPS if location is unavailable.
3. Keep location permission enabled only as required by your organization's policy. Do not enter someone else's attendance.
4. Check out when the control is available at the end of your workday.

## Customer visits and calls

1. Select a planned visit, confirm the customer and purpose, and start the visit. Allow GPS if appropriate.
2. Complete the visit with the actual outcome, notes, products, and any expected-order details supported by the customer discussion.
3. Add the next follow-up date when one is agreed. Do not enter an estimated order value as a confirmed sale.
4. For an unplanned customer visit, use the unplanned visit action and enter the actual date, purpose, and priority.
5. Log calls against the correct customer and summarize the discussion and next action.

## Voice notes and transcription

1. Use the microphone control where available. Allow microphone permission only when needed.
2. Confirm you have permission to record and transcribe the conversation before recording.
3. Review the transcript for names, dates, amounts, and technical terms before saving it.
4. Do not record sensitive personal information that is not needed for CRM work. If transcription fails, enter a short written note instead.

## Leads and follow-ups

1. Open the lead pipeline and create a lead with the customer's correct details and source.
2. Move the lead to a stage only when the corresponding business event has occurred. Review the saved stage history after the change. If supporting detail is needed, log an activity against the lead.
3. Create a follow-up with a clear action, customer, owner, and due date.
4. Mark a follow-up complete only after the action is done. Update the due date if the customer agrees to a later date.

## Support requests and daily review

1. Use the Requests area for sample, quotation, technical support, complaint, or other work that needs coordination.
2. Include the customer, concise requirement, responsible person, and due date when known.
3. Before signing out, review today's visits, open follow-ups, and requests. Correct incomplete records while details are fresh.
4. Ask your RM if a customer is missing, assigned incorrectly, or unavailable in your account; do not create a duplicate to work around it.

## Good data practice

- CRM activity records operational work; Tally invoices are the source for accounting sales figures.
- Ask AI can summarize only the data available to your role. Verify important decisions in the CRM or Tally.
- Report missing customer mappings, stale sync indicators, or unexpected values to your manager/admin.
- Never send customer data outside approved company channels.

## Phase 7 practical reminders and exercises

- **My Day** is the normal landing screen. Use **Sales Hub** for customer calls, leads, requests and alerts. Save call direction, actual time and a short result; the CRM does not prove a telephone call occurred.
- If location is denied or unavailable, check-in can still save attendance. Tell your manager that GPS is missing; do not enter invented coordinates. End-of-day checkout records attendance closure; verify the saved time. A GPS location at checkout is not guaranteed.
- After saving a visit, use the optional voice-note prompt if needed. Uploaded audio and an edited transcript are separate records. If the upload fails, retain a written summary and report the error. Transcription may use browser recognition or a configured paid provider; it was not live-tested in this phase.
- Enter a valid lead next-action date and nonnegative expected value. Lead next-action dates do not automatically create follow-up reminder rows. Create a separate follow-up if the task needs to appear in the follow-up list.
- Alerts show pending/due-today follow-ups, upcoming visits and past scheduled visits requiring confirmation. These are in-app reminders, not proof of an external message.
- Review the saved daily summary before signing out. Expected orders and lead values are opportunities; accounting sales come from Tally.

Practice in an approved training environment: sign in, check in, start and complete a visit, save one call, correct a transcript, create and advance a lead, schedule a follow-up, raise a support request, inspect the daily summary and check out. Confirm all saved records before signing out. Do not use actual customer recordings for the exercise.
