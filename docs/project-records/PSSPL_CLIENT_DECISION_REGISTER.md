Assessment date: 18 October 2026. Branch: `feature/psspl-phase3-rm-report-cards`.

This register separates five unimplemented policy/data requirements from five additional, separately classified NOT VERIFIED gates. “Recommended” options are requests for a decision, not approvals. No client choice is assumed. The approving parties are role labels drawn from the existing client approval checklist; the named business owner there is Palak Ma'am.
